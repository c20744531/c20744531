## 💫 About Me

I'm a **data analyst, web developer, UX/UI designer, video editor and graphic designer**. Yes, that's a lot of hats, but they all come down to the same thing: turning messy, scattered stuff into something clear and enjoyable to use.

### What I do

- 📊 **Data analysis**: digging through numbers until they tell a story worth listening to
- 💻 **Web development**: building sites and tools that are fast, clean and easy to use
- 🎨 **UX/UI design**: designing interfaces that make sense the first time you see them
- 🎬 **Video editing**: cutting footage so it hits the right beats
- 🖌️ **Graphic design**: visuals with a purpose, not just decoration

### How I work

I'm very creative, but I'm also the person who notices the 2px misalignment and the one wrong number in row 400. I care about the big picture and the tiny details equally, because the small stuff is usually what makes something feel polished.

My background in **finance, fraud analysis and auditing** means I'm used to being careful, asking "does this add up?" and not trusting something just because it looks right.
